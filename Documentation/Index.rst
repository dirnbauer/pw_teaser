=============================================================
Page Teaser (with Fluid)
=============================================================

:Extension key: pw_teaser
:Package name: t3/pw_teaser
:Version: 8.1.0
:Language: en
:License: This document is published under the
   `Open Content License <https://www.openhub.net/licenses/opl>`__.
:Rendered: |today|

Create powerful, dynamic page teasers with data from page properties and their
content elements, based on Extbase and the Fluid template engine.

This manual documents pw_teaser 8.1 on TYPO3 13.4 LTS and 14.3 LTS with
PHP 8.3 or newer.

**Features**

- Five page sources: children of this page, recursive trees or hand-picked pages
- Category filter with AND, OR and NOT logic
- Ordering by title, dates, manual sorting, custom fields or random
- Three template modes (preset, file, directory) for Fluid templates
- Built-in pagination (``SimplePagination``, optional
  ``georgringer/numbered-pagination``)
- PSR-14 ``ModifyPagesEvent`` to filter, sort or enrich the result
